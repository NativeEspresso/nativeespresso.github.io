Privacy Policy

Effective 15 November 2026 · Trans-Title for Android

1. Your media and audio are processed 100% on your device

Trans-Title generates subtitles and transcribes audio completely offline using on-device machine learning models. Your media files, recordings, transcriptions, and subtitle texts are never uploaded, transmitted, logged, or shared by us. Processing functions in full without any network connection (including in airplane mode).

2. Third-party services in the free version

If the app displays advertisements or offers in-app purchases, standard certified SDKs are utilized:

  • Google AdMob — may collect device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID), coarse IP address, and interaction metrics to deliver non-personalized or rewarded ads. See Google’s Privacy Policy. Pro upgrades permanently remove ads.
  • Google Play Billing — in-app purchases and upgrades are handled securely by Google Play. We do not receive or store payment or credit card details.

3. Data we collect directly

None. Trans-Title operates without user accounts, cloud databases, or behavioral tracking SDKs. We do not collect or monitor your activity.

4. Optional feedback & reports

If you choose to submit feedback or crash reports via email, only the information you explicitly provide is received to troubleshoot issues. It is never sold or used for marketing.

5. Children’s Privacy

Trans-Title is a general utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Any updates will be reflected with a revised effective date on this page.

7. Contact & inquiries

Native Espresso — native.espresso@gmail.com.

8. How to request data deletion

Because Trans-Title stores no accounts, telemetry, or files on remote servers, your data exists solely on your personal device. Deleting the application removes all local app data. To delete any prior email support correspondence, email native.espresso@gmail.com.
