Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Trans-Title upload my audio, videos, or transcripts?

No. Trans-Title is built on an absolute privacy architecture. All speech-to-text recognition, subtitle translation, and video processing runs 100% on your Android hardware. Your media never leaves your device and the app functions fully in airplane mode.

What are the 4 accuracy tiers in AI Transcribe?

We provide four optimized on-device models: Fair (ultra-lightweight for quick notes), Better (balanced for everyday dialogue), High (high-precision for multi-speaker discussions), and Superior (maximum recognition fidelity for studio work, gated safely for devices with ≥ 5 GB RAM).

Can it transcribe long videos and podcasts without cutting off words?

Yes. Trans-Title uses 5-minute continuous pipelining combined with a 3-second overlap stitching algorithm that ensures words at chunk boundaries are never truncated or duplicated.

Does transcription continue when the screen is locked?

Yes. Trans-Title runs a resilient foreground media service with CPU wake-lock protection so you can lock your screen or switch apps without interrupting the process.

What is Cross-Play Studio?

Cross-Play is our video workbench that lets you pair a video container with external dubbed audio tracks (with positive or negative millisecond delay adjustment for lip-sync) and subtitle sidecars, allowing you to preview or export multi-track media with ease.

How do I become an official closed tester?

Click "Join Closed Testing Queue" or email native.espresso@gmail.com with your Google Play Store email address. We will add you to the Google Play Closed Testing track immediately.
