Does Trans-Title upload my audio, videos, or transcripts?

No. Trans-Title is built on an absolute privacy architecture. All speech-to-text recognition, subtitle translation, and video processing runs 100% on your hardware. Your media never leaves your device and the app functions fully in airplane mode.

What are the 4 accuracy tiers in AI Transcribe?

We provide four optimized on-device accuracy levels: Fair (ultra-lightweight for quick notes), Better (balanced for everyday dialogue), High (high-precision for multi-speaker discussions), and Superior (maximum recognition fidelity for studio work, safely gated for devices with ≥ 5 GB RAM).

Can it transcribe long videos and podcasts without cutting off words?

Yes. Trans-Title uses 5-minute continuous pipelining combined with a 3-second overlap stitching algorithm that ensures words at chunk boundaries are never truncated or duplicated.

Does transcription continue when the screen is locked?

Yes. Trans-Title runs a resilient foreground media service with wake-lock protection so you can lock your screen or switch apps without interrupting progress.

What is Cross-Play Studio?

Cross-Play is our video workbench that lets you pair a video container with external dubbed audio tracks (with positive or negative millisecond delay adjustment for lip-sync) and subtitle sidecars, allowing you to preview or export multi-track media effortlessly.

What is the desktop & multi-platform roadmap (Windows, iOS, macOS, Linux)?

After testing an early JavaFX player prototype, we moved desktop engineering to native WinUI 3 on Windows for superior GPU smoothness and system integration (currently in active development alongside Android Closed Testing). Native iOS and macOS editions will follow once dedicated Apple Mac build hardware is provisioned, with a Linux edition planned as studio bandwidth permits.
