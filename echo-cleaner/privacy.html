Privacy Policy

Effective 15 November 2026 · Echo Cleaner for Android

1. Your audio is processed 100% on your device

Echo Cleaner enhances audio (echo removal, noise reduction, de-reverb, voice boost) entirely offline using FFmpeg DSP and on-device AI models (DeepFilterNet and the Clear model) running through ONNX Runtime. Your recordings, enhanced files, and their contents are never uploaded, transmitted, logged, or shared by us. Processing works without any network connection.

2. Third-party services in the free version

The free version is ad-supported and uses Google Play Billing for the Pro upgrade. These services may collect data under their own policies:

  • Google AdMob — may collect the advertising ID, IP address, device information, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads (including rewarded ads). See Google’s Privacy Policy and AdMob’s data disclosure. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings, and Pro removes ads entirely.
  • Google Play Billing — purchases are processed by Google; we receive purchase status, not your payment details.

3. Data we collect directly

None. Echo Cleaner currently has no accounts, no analytics SDK, and no server-side collection of your data. The only data leaving your device is what the third-party services above handle as described.

4. Optional feedback (when introduced)

A future version may offer an optional form to report problems. If you choose to use it, only what you explicitly submit (message, optional contact email, optional screenshots) will be sent to our backend hosted on Microsoft Azure and stored in Azure database/storage. It is never required, never includes your audio files, and you may request deletion at any time via the contact below. This policy will be updated before that feature ships.

5. Children

Echo Cleaner is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. The effective date above will change, and material changes will be noted in the app’s release notes.

7. Contact & deletion requests

Talha Amjad — ch.talha.amjad@outlook.com. For any privacy question or deletion request, email us; we respond within a reasonable time.

8. How to request data deletion

  1. Email ch.talha.amjad@outlook.com with the subject “Data deletion request”, including any email address or screenshots you submitted.
  2. We delete feedback messages, contact details and screenshots stored on our Azure backend within 30 days and confirm by email.
  3. We hold nothing else: your audio never leaves your device, and we keep no accounts or analytics.

Ad identifiers (Advertising ID) are processed by Google, not us — delete them on-device via Android Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID. Retention: feedback data is kept only to address your report and deleted on request (maximum 24 months); no other data is retained.
