Frequently Asked Questions

Does Echo Cleaner upload my audio?

No. All enhancement runs 100% on-device using our proprietary neural audio engines. Processing works cleanly in airplane mode.

How does the free tier work?

One free enhancement per day, plus two extra unlocks by watching a rewarded ad. Pro removes limits and ads.

How does the enhancement engine work?

Echo Cleaner processes audio through a multi-stage acoustic pipeline: acoustic echo cancellation, on-device neural denoising, room de-reverberation, and hardware-accelerated dynamic loudness shaping — with switchable Clean Room and Studio Pro engines.

Is my purchase tied to an account?

No account needed. Pro is handled by Google Play Billing and restores automatically with your Play account.

How do I report a problem?

Use the Support portal above or email native.espresso@gmail.com. We only receive what you explicitly submit.
