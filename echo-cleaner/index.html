Questions

FAQ

Does Echo Cleaner upload my audio?

No. All enhancement runs 100% on-device using our proprietary neural audio engine. Processing works fully in airplane mode.

How does the free tier work?

One free enhancement per day, plus two extra unlocks by watching a rewarded ad. Pro removes limits and ads.

How does the enhancement engine work?

Echo Cleaner processes audio through a multi-stage acoustic pipeline: acoustic echo cancellation, deep neural denoising, acoustic room de-reverberation, and hardware-accelerated dynamic range loudness shaping.

Is my purchase tied to an account?

No account needed. Pro is handled by Google Play Billing and restores with your Play account.

How do I report a problem?

Email native.espresso@gmail.com. An in-app feedback form is coming; it will only send what you explicitly submit.
