The Native Espresso Standard

Built for users, not surveillance.

⛨

100% On-Device Processing

AI models and DSP routines execute locally on your device's CPU, GPU, and NPU. Your audio, video, and personal files never get beamed to external clouds.

⚡

Native Bare-Metal Speed

We write performant C++, Rust, and assembly kernels where cycles count, connected to clean Kotlin & Jetpack Compose interfaces for buttery 120Hz responsiveness.

✕

No Surveillance or Data Brokers

No behavioral tracking SDKs. No telemetry spying on what files you process. We don't want your personal data, and we don't build systems that can hold it.

✈

Airplane Mode as Default

Apps should never brick themselves when you enter a tunnel, board a flight, or lose signal. Core capabilities remain fully operational offline.

✦

Transparent & Fair Model

No predatory subscriptions with hidden renewal traps. Free tiers with clear limitations, and clean one-time Pro purchases powered by Google Play Billing.

✉

Direct Engineering Support

Built by an independent developer in Sargodha, Pakistan. You communicate directly with the engineer who builds the code, not an outsourced support queue.

Under The Hood

How our architecture works

  1. 01

    On-Device Neural Inference
